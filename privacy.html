Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. Introduction

FinSight ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our financial dashboard service.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Account Information

  • Name: Your display name for account identification
  • Email: Used for account verification and communication
  • Password Hash: Securely hashed using industry-standard algorithms

2.2 Financial Data

  • Account Balances: Your account balances and transaction history
  • Transactions: Records of your financial transactions
  • Categories: Your expense and income categories
  • Goals: Your financial goals and targets

2.3 Technical Data

  • Session Tokens: HTTP-only secure cookies for authentication
  • IP Address: Temporarily logged for security and rate limiting
  • Device Information: Browser type and version for compatibility

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Provide and maintain the financial dashboard service
  • Authenticate your account and secure your sessions
  • Calculate account balances and financial metrics
  • Send essential communications (verification emails, security alerts)
  • Prevent fraud and protect against unauthorized access
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption: All passwords are hashed using bcrypt
  • Secure Sessions: HTTP-only, same-origin cookies
  • Database Security: User-scoped queries and soft deletion
  • Rate Limiting: Protection against brute force attacks
  • HTTPS: All data transmitted over encrypted connections
  • Password Checks: Integration with Have I Been Pwned API

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account:

  • All personal information is permanently removed
  • Financial data is deleted or anonymized
  • Audit logs may be retained for security purposes

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Have I Been Pwned API: To check password security (no passwords are sent)
  • Sharesansar: To fetch public NEPSE market data
  • Email Providers: To send verification emails (configurable)

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your data
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies for:

  • Session authentication (HTTP-only, secure)
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Rate limiting

We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

9. Children's Privacy

FinSight is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data is stored in your chosen database location. By using FinSight, you consent to the processing of your data in the location where your database is hosted.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at our contact page.
