Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: October 24, 2026

FinSight is a personal financial dashboard that helps you track accounts, monitor transactions, analyze cash flow, and manage your investments in one secure place. It's designed for individuals and small businesses who want better visibility into their finances.
Yes. We use industry-standard security measures including bcrypt password hashing, HTTP-only secure cookies, user-scoped database queries, and rate limiting to protect your data. All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Learn more in our Privacy Policy.
Simply create an account with your email and a secure password (minimum 12 characters). After email verification, you'll have access to a pre-configured dashboard with a Cash account and starter expense categories. You can then add more accounts, import transactions, and customize your categories.
CSV import functionality is planned for a future release. Currently, you can manually add transactions or use the API to programmatically import your data. Contact us if you need assistance with data migration.
Yes, FinSight is currently free to use. We may introduce premium features in the future, but core financial tracking will remain free. Your data is yours - you can export it at any time.
FinSight works on all modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. We recommend using the latest version of your browser for the best experience and security.
Yes, FinSight is fully responsive and works on mobile devices, tablets, and desktop computers. The interface adapts to your screen size for optimal viewing.
Your data is stored in your own database. In local development, this is SQLite. For production deployment, we recommend PostgreSQL. You control where your data lives - self-host or use a cloud provider of your choice.
When you delete your account, all your personal information and financial data are permanently removed from the database. This action cannot be undone. We recommend exporting your data before deletion if you want to keep a copy.
